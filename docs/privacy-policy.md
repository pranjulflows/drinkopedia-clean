# Drinkopedia privacy policy

_Effective 12 September 2026_

Drinkopedia is a reference app about spirits, liqueurs, wine and beer. It is
built to work without knowing anything about you. This policy explains the
little data it handles, and where that data goes.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Drinkopedia has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no
analytics and no crash reporting. We do not collect, store, sell or share any
personal information, and we have no server of our own that the app reports
to.

## What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone so it can work well. None of them is
ever sent to us or to anyone else.

- **Your taste picks.** The drink categories you choose when you first open
  the app, and whether you have finished that intro. They are used only to
  decide which spirits appear first.
- **A cache of spirit information and bottle images**, so pages load quickly
  and stay readable offline.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings,
deletes all of it.

## Services the app connects to

To show spirit information and bottle images, the app requests them from
[TheCocktailDB](https://www.thecocktaildb.com). Those requests ask for a
spirit by its name or its catalogue id and carry no information about you.
Like any request on the internet, they reveal your device's IP address and
basic technical details, such as the app's HTTP client, to TheCocktailDB's
servers. How TheCocktailDB handles that is covered by its own policies.

The app is downloaded and updated through Google Play, which is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

Drinkopedia is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed
at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children
included.

## Changes

If the app's handling of data ever changes, this policy will be updated first
and the effective date above will change with it.
