Drinkopedia is a pocket guide to liquor and everything else behind the bar: spirits, liqueurs, wine and beer. Look up what's in your glass, then read where it came from and how it's made, written out in full rather than squeezed onto a recipe card.

It's a reference, not a cocktail recipe app. Open Bourbon and you get the story of American whiskey: what it's distilled from, why it's aged in barrels and where the name comes from. Open Amaretto and find out why a sweet almond liqueur is named after the Italian word for "a little bitter".

WHAT'S ON THE SHELF
Whiskey, bourbon, rye and Scotch whisky. Gin, rum, vodka, tequila and mezcal. Brandy, cognac and calvados. Liqueurs, from amaretto and Chartreuse to crème de menthe. Vermouth and aperitifs like Campari and Aperol. Absinthe, sake, ouzo, pisco and cachaça. Wine, Champagne, sherry, port, beer and cider.

THE HISTORY BEHIND THE BOTTLE
Why is bourbon aged in charred oak? What has to happen before a whisky can be called Scotch? Why was absinthe banned, and where does the blue agave in tequila grow? The origin stories in Drinkopedia answer questions like these, so you know what's in your glass and where each liquor came from.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Read the origin story: the history, the production and the name behind each drink, wherever there's one to tell.
• See the type and the alcohol by volume (ABV) where they're known. Nothing is guessed: a figure that isn't known isn't shown.
• Filter the shelf in one tap: Whiskey, Rum, Gin, Vodka, Tequila & mezcal, Brandy, Liqueur, Wine, Beer & cider or Aperitif.
• Search by name or by type. Try "rum", "vermouth" or "Scotch".
• Pick the categories you're into when you first open the app, and they come first on your shelf. Everything else stays one search away.
• Keep reading offline: spirits you've already browsed stay available without a connection.
• Dark mode that follows your phone's setting.

MADE TO BE READ
A loud, sticker-style design in acid green, hot pink and electric blue, with big type and bottle artwork on every card. The stories are the point, so story pages are built for reading: large text, room to breathe, and a header that gets out of the way as you scroll.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
There's no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. The categories you pick are saved on your phone and never leave it.

Available in English and French.
